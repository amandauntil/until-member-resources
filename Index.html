Booking & App
Open the UNTIL app → tap Book a Space → select your date, time and room type. All sessions must be booked before you or your client arrives. Credits are deducted from your balance at the time of booking.
When making or editing a booking in the app, there is a guest field — add your client's name and email address. Every client must be registered before they arrive. On their first visit they'll complete a guest check-in form at the desk. Failure to register guests may result in access being refused.
In the UNTIL app, go to your account → Credits → Buy credits. Select the credit pack that suits you. Credits are applied to your account immediately. Note: credits do not roll over between monthly billing periods.
Yes — all members can use the gym, but this requires FOH approval on the day and must be booked with the club team. It is not self-serve through the app. Please check with FOH before entering the gym outside of a booked client session. The gym must be under 60% occupancy. Misuse without approval will result in a written warning.
Salto is UNTIL's digital key system. When you book a space, Salto automatically unlocks that room for your booking window. Hold your phone to the Salto reader on the door to enter. You only have access to the spaces you have booked — access expires at the end of your session. If you're having issues with access, speak to FOH immediately.
Yes. Your membership gives you multisite access across all four UNTIL clubs — Soho, Marylebone, Liverpool Street, and Canary Wharf. Simply book through the app and select your preferred club location.
Membership & Billing
Your membership renews monthly on the date your membership started. You can check your renewal date in the UNTIL app under your account settings. Payment is taken automatically via direct debit.
You can pause your membership through the UNTIL app. Go to your account → Membership → Pause. Pauses are subject to the terms in your membership agreement. If you're unsure, contact your club team directly before making any changes.
Cancellation requests must be submitted through the app or in writing to your club team. Notice periods apply as per your membership T&Cs. Please review your agreement before cancelling — we'd also love to understand why, so if there's anything we can help with, speak to your Community Lead first.
Storage is available at all four clubs on a monthly rolling basis and is prioritised for members doing 10+ hours per week. Prices vary by club and storage type (lockers, floor space, medical fridge, dental cupboards). Contact your club GM to check availability and pricing. Storage is invoiced separately from your membership.
Space & Etiquette
Filming on the gym floor requires prior permission from the site team on each occasion. Treatment and coach rooms can be booked for content filming — please let your GM know if you're bringing non-members in. Dental surgery filming also requires club team permission for any public space filming. General filming of other members or clients is strictly prohibited without their consent.
Tell FOH immediately. Don't just clean it up yourself — we need to log it and follow up with the previous member. This protects you (it won't be attributed to you) and helps us maintain standards across the club.
On the gym floor, sessions end on time regardless of late arrivals — the team cannot extend if it impacts the next booking. In treatment and coach rooms, contact the team as soon as possible and we'll try to move you to another available room or extend where we can. For dental surgeries, sessions always end on time.
Report any breakages, damage, or missing items to FOH immediately. This applies to gym equipment, room fixtures, dental instruments, and anything else in the club. We don't expect perfection — accidents happen — but transparency matters.
Community & Growth
Events are shared in the bi-weekly member newsletter, the WhatsApp community, and via the club teams. All events are hosted on Luma — find our events at lu.ma/until. If you'd like to speak at, co-host, or suggest an event, speak to your Community Lead.
Health Concierge is UNTIL's client-matching service, run by Hayley. If you're ready to take on new clients, Hayley will match UNTIL members and prospective clients to practitioners based on specialism, availability and location. To get set up, speak to your Community Lead or contact Hayley directly through the club team.
Growth Services is UNTIL's practitioner business growth programme, run by James Morrissey. It includes a free Business Audit (your visibility on Google, AI tools, and social), Website & SEO from £150/month, and a Business Mastermind cohort. To get started with a free audit, speak to your Community Lead or any club team member.
